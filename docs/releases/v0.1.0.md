# Ferese 0.1.0

Ferese 0.1.0 is our first stable release. It includes the window manager, panel,
Settings, notifications and lock screen, with one set of appearance settings
across the desktop.

## Arrange your windows

Choose a layout for each workspace. Scroll through columns of windows, tile them
to fit the screen, or let them float. You can switch layouts as your work changes
and group related windows into tabs.

Move and resize windows with the mouse or keyboard. Overview shows your open
windows and workspaces, and the launcher finds apps, files and commands.
Display scaling has finer steps for getting text to a comfortable size.

## Change the panel and appearance

Build your panel in Settings. Move controls between groups, put the panel at the
top or bottom, and choose anything from a full-width bar to separate floating
controls. Morph keeps the panel in a small hub that opens when you need it.

Pick from six themes with light and dark versions, then adjust the colors,
wallpaper, transparency and motion. The shell, Settings, menus and lock screen
follow the same theme. Changes apply as you make them.

The panel can also show command and build progress, music controls, and a focus
timer with work and break sessions.

## Share and record

Share a window, a display, or one workspace. A shared workspace stays on screen
for your audience when you switch elsewhere, and leaves out the panel and
notifications. Sharing ends when you lock the desktop.

Record a display or a selected area from the panel, with optional microphone
and system audio. The new **Capture status** item shows which apps are sharing.
With the optional OBS connection set up, it also shows when you're live,
recording, paused or reconnecting. An OBS preview alone shows **Sharing**.

Your saved panel layout stays as it is. To add the new indicator, open
**Settings → Panel & Shell → Edit panel → Add item** and choose **Capture status**.
The [sharing guide](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/screen-sharing.md#panel-status)
includes the OBS setup steps.

We removed repeated background checks and work that redraws an unchanged screen.
Fixes also cover floating windows opening on the wrong monitor and notification
menus closing when their unread badge clears.

## Install or upgrade

Packages are available below for Fedora 44 and Arch Linux on 64-bit Intel and
AMD PCs. Other distributions can [build from source](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/installation.md).

If you're moving from a source installation to a package, follow the
[package installation guide](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/packaging.md#install-a-release-package)
first. Keep your personal configuration, then log out and back in after updating.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/issues)
with what happened, how to reproduce it, and your Ferese version.
