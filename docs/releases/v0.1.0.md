# Ferese 0.1.0

Ferese 0.1.0 is our first stable release. The window manager, panel, Settings,
notifications and lock screen come together as one desktop, with appearance
settings that carry through all of them.

You might want a wide editor and terminal in scrolling columns, then a different
workspace where every window fits on screen. Ferese lets you choose a layout for
each workspace and switch it as you go. Windows can float or sit together in tabs,
and you can move and resize them with the mouse or keyboard. Overview helps you
find your way back to an open window. For apps, files and commands, there's the
launcher. Display scaling also has finer steps to help get text to a comfortable
size.

The panel is yours to arrange in Settings. Put it at the top or bottom, move its
controls between groups, or try Morph if you'd rather have a small hub that opens
when you need it. A full-width bar and separate floating controls are available
too. It can show music controls, command and build progress, or a focus timer with
work and break sessions. Pick one of six themes, each with light and dark versions,
then adjust the wallpaper, colors, transparency and motion. Those changes apply
as you make them.

Sharing a workspace is useful when you want to show what you're working on and
still move elsewhere on your own screen. Your audience stays with the workspace
you chose, while its panel and notifications stay out of the picture. You can also
share a window or a whole display. Locking the desktop ends sharing. To save a
video yourself, record a display or a selected area from the panel, with optional
microphone and system audio.

There's a new **Capture status** item for keeping track of that sharing. If you
connect it to OBS, it can also tell you when you're live, recording, paused or
reconnecting. An OBS preview by itself shows **Sharing**. Existing panel layouts
stay as they are, so add the item through **Settings → Panel & Shell → Edit panel →
Add item** if you want it. The [sharing guide](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/screen-sharing.md#panel-status)
has the OBS setup steps.

We also removed repeated background checks and work that redraws an unchanged
screen. Fixes cover floating windows opening on the wrong monitor and notification
menus closing when their unread badge clears.

## Getting started

Download the Fedora 44 or Arch Linux package below for a 64-bit Intel or AMD PC.
Other distributions can [build from source](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/installation.md).

If you're moving from a source installation to a package, read the
[installation guide](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/blob/v0.1.0/docs/packaging.md#install-a-release-package)
first. Keep your personal configuration, then log out and back in after updating.

If something goes wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/ferese-wm/ferese/issues)
with your Ferese version, what happened, and how to reproduce it.
